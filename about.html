Our Founder

Pandiyammal Naganathan grew up watching elders use turmeric, Kasturi manjal, nalangu maavu, and herbal bath powders as part of everyday skincare.

Over time, she noticed many people shifting to chemical-heavy products and facing irritation, sensitivity, and long-term skin problems. This inspired her to create Ponviyal — a brand families can trust and safely pass on to the next generation.

Our Mission

To craft honest, affordable herbal skincare that solves real problems like sensitivity, dullness, and everyday skin damage using time-tested Indian ingredients.

Every Ponviyal product stays close to homemade traditions while maintaining modern hygiene, consistency, and quality.

Our Story

Before Ponviyal, many customers struggled with itching, rashes, dryness, and irritation caused by harsh soaps and cosmetics filled with parabens, sulfates, and synthetic fragrances.

Traditional recipes existed in homes but were not available in a consistent, hygienic, branded form. Ponviyal was created to bridge this gap with minimal, transparent ingredients rooted in generations of Indian wisdom.

Our Vision

To grow Ponviyal into a trusted herbal brand from Tamil Nadu, known across India for purity, respect for tradition, and genuine results on Indian skin.

We aim to reconnect people with heritage bath and beauty rituals while supporting ethical sourcing, conscious living, and small farming communities.

Growing across Tamil Nadu.

At Ponviyal, we believe that true beauty begins with nature’s purity. Born from the vision of SJM Herbal Products, our purpose is to bring back the trusted Indian herbal traditions that have cared for skin for generations. We combine these age-old practices with clean and modern formulations made for today’s lifestyle. Our Ponmanjal collection features authentic Kasturi Manjal, known for its natural glow-enhancing benefits. Every ingredient is selected with care, ensuring purity, safety, and timeless wellness. With every product, we honor our roots and help you glow confidently and naturally.
